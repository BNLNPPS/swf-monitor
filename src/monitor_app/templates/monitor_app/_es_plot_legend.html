Reading the plot. One lane per core. Green processing, red failed, grey idle; after a sudden end, light yellow work cut off, dark yellow output lost on the node. White lines split the units; hover over one for its events and the close that shipped it.

Blue: output handling. Each close merges the finished units into one file, ships it off the node and registers it, then credits the events in PanDA. Short, regular closes mean a sudden end loses little.
