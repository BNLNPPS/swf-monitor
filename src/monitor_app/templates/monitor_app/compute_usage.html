Period boundaries are Eastern time. Jobs are attributed by end time. The OSG pool is one PanDA queue spanning many sites: its row expands into the sites its jobs ran at in the selected period, the glidein site the pilot reports on each job record (or, for older records, the worker-node host's site); a job that never left the submit host is listed as not dispatched, and a site's share is its share of the pool. Allocated core-hours are allocated cores multiplied by wall time for every job that ran, finished or failed; wasted core-hours are the part held by failed jobs; productive core-hours are the CPU consumed by finished jobs, and efficiency is productive over allocated, so every failure counts against it. Failed jobs are counted by end time.
