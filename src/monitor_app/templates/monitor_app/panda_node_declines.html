Node declines

A decline is the payload refusing a bad landing in its first seconds and exiting rather than spending a slot it cannot use: the node could not reach what the job needs. Every node that declined in the window is listed with what it produced in the same window, so a busy node is not read as a bad one, and with its own account of why where the job's report has been filed. Nothing here bans a node; a person does that, on this evidence.
